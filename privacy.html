Privacy information

How Wrestling Manager handles information in the current beta.

Beta information · September 20, 2026

The final privacy policy and complete account-deletion process are still being prepared before the athlete pilot. This page explains current handling and how to contact us with a privacy question.

Information used by the app

Depending on the features you use, information may include account and contact details, profile photos, birthdays, guardian relationships, school or grade, clothing sizes, team memberships and roles; weights and weigh-ins; attendance, events, challenges and scored matches; messages and attachments; medical or clearance documents; staff credentials, officials' assignments, budgets, signed forms and feedback.

You, a connected guardian or authorized team staff may provide information. The app can also receive a connected scale reading or scan an athlete credential. Account, session and notification-device identifiers and service logs help operate and protect the service.

Why it is used

Information supports sign-in, family connections, team access, rosters, calendars, wrestling records, permitted communications and notifications, safety review, troubleshooting and feedback.

Who can access it

Access depends on assigned roles, teams, divisions, family connections and feature permissions. Coaches and administrators have access for their authorized responsibilities. Shared Team Device and Test Athlete logins have limited access.

Authorized organization operations roles can access relevant roster information and guardian phone numbers within their assigned scope even when ordinary coach contact sharing is turned off. That operations roster excludes athlete phone numbers and email addresses, private weight history and medical documents.

Messages and attachments are visible to permitted recipients and, when safety rules apply, connected guardians and authorized safety reviewers. Information published to affiliated clubs is visible to the relevant clubs. Anyone with a calendar subscription link may be able to read the events it contains; keep the link private.

Services that handle information

  • Supabase handles accounts, the database, stored files and backend processing.
  • GitHub Pages and jsDelivr deliver the web app and its client library. Requests to hosting services include technical information such as an IP address.
  • Resend delivers enabled app emails and receives the recipient address and email content.
  • Apple delivers enabled iPhone/iPad push notifications using device tokens and alert content. Depending on notification choices, weigh-in alerts can include an athlete's name and weight on the device's lock screen.
  • Google Workspace handles messages you choose to send to our support email address.

SMS is not ready for this pilot. The currently registered push devices use Apple notifications. We will update this information when additional communication services are enabled.

Device permissions and local data

Camera, photo, Bluetooth and notification permissions support the features you choose to use. You can change device permissions in device Settings. The app also keeps sign-in preferences, settings and unfinished match recovery information locally on your device.

Retention and account deletion

A season ending does not automatically erase its records. Membership and season dates are separate from data-retention rules. Signing out, uninstalling the app or leaving a team does not by itself delete stored account or athlete information.

A complete in-app account-deletion workflow is not available yet. Contact support with a data request so its identity, scope and any shared family records can be reviewed. Sending an email does not mean deletion has been completed. Retention periods and the final deletion process must be settled before the athlete pilot.

A request to remove a guardian's account is different from a request about a child's records. A child's profile may be shared with another guardian or multiple teams; the appropriate authority and scope must be verified.

Your choices and questions

You can edit permitted profile information and adjust available contact-sharing, notification and guardian communication settings. Team access and guardian chat controls do not replace any consent needed for collecting a younger child's information; younger-athlete enrollment arrangements must be finalized before the pilot.

For questions, corrections or requests concerning your or your child's information, contact support@theteammanager.app.
