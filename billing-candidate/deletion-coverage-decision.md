# Approved paid-team deletion behavior

Owner approved October 3, 2026: if the purchaser deletes their personal account, preserve Team Pro only through the already paid period, provided another authorized administrator remains. Family coverage ends with its owner.

Implemented server policy and database candidates: team-paid-remainder.mjs, team-paid-remainder-storage.sql, team-paid-remainder-postgres.mjs and billing-notification-persistence.mjs. The private grant stores team/environment/plan/paid boundary/revocation/version plus a pseudonymous SHA-256 binding of environment, original transaction and the random app-account token. Neither raw Apple identifier, purchaser ID, receipt nor athlete link is copied. This hash is a private refund lookup, not a claim of anonymization. Current remaining-admin authority is required when creating and using the grant. Later renewals cannot extend the paid boundary. Verified refunds revoke access permanently. Expired or billing-retry status cannot preserve access past the current time.

Canonical remaining-admin permission must follow the live public.is_team_admin definition inspected October 3: active head coach, active assistant coach/manager with team_admin permission, or organization_admin membership. Reject deleted/banned/managed/deleting identities and deleted team/organization.

The full-worker candidate prepares the paid grant before erasing sealed billing records, in the same database transaction. The records-to-auth transition remains a cleanup backstop. Grant creation and purchaser cleanup roll back together on any error. The reviewed deletion planner covers subscriptions, delivery records, paid and unpaid intents, family coverage, team bindings and matching notifications. Already acknowledged inbox refunds are applied before those notifications are erased. Future verified notifications match the private hash and update the grant without recreating raw-token inbox entries. The access resolver checks live remaining-admin authority and exact expiry. A bounded cleanup function removes expired grants, and team deletion removes them through a foreign key. Database guards forbid extending a paid boundary, rebinding a grant, rolling back its version or clearing its revocation.

Seven isolated PostgreSQL scenarios exercise permissions, partial-failure rollback, personal cleanup, ordinary member access, post-deletion refund/renewal handling, acknowledged pending refunds, expiry cleanup and team deletion. A surviving administrator's shared photo is retained when their team staff row is deleted. The Auth/Storage providers and adult eligibility fixture are synthetic. These tests do not establish concurrent hosted deletion or actual Apple receipt acceptance.
