No account or app installation required. This option is for a coach-verified parent or legal guardian of an athlete aged 13–17.

  • Your teen can update their bio, goals, affiliation, age division, tournament results and entrance music.

You can withdraw this permission later through a fresh email link below. Joining later puts your account’s parent controls in charge. This choice does not replace required consent for children under 13 or other legally required permissions.

Allow team messaging with an adult reviewer

This is separate from profile permission. Your teen can use team chats, coach-led groups and direct conversations with authorized coaches or team leaders. The named reviewer is included on every message alongside a different authorized adult. Athlete-to-athlete private chats are not included.

The reviewer can read messages and shared media without routine alerts. Their name is visible in the conversation. Other currently authorized team reviewers retain their review access. This does not make anyone your child’s guardian.

Permission lasts up to one year and stops being effective if eligibility or the approved reviewer assignment changes. You can withdraw it through an emailed management link. Team rules, safety filters and reporting duties still apply.
