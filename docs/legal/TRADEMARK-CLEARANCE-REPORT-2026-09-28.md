# Trademark Clearance Report — Preliminary

Date: 2026-09-28  
Marks reviewed: **THE TEAM MANAGER** and **WRESTLING MANAGER**

This is a preliminary business/legal-risk review, not an attorney opinion or a completed USPTO clearance search.

## Findings

### THE TEAM MANAGER

No obvious identical current sports-team-management software conflict was located in the initial internet and indexed federal-source search performed for this audit. However, the sports-team-management market contains numerous "team manager" formative names and products. "The Team Manager" may therefore face descriptiveness/weak-mark issues depending on the goods/services wording and examiner analysis.

Recommended positioning: use **THE TEAM MANAGER™** consistently as the umbrella/source brand, preferably with a distinctive logo and trade dress, and avoid presenting "team manager" generically as the sole source identifier.

### WRESTLING MANAGER

The phrase is already in current commercial use by multiple wrestling-management/simulation applications and has historical use in tournament-management software. Because the wording directly describes management software related to wrestling, it is likely to receive narrower protection and may be difficult to register broadly without acquired distinctiveness or a distinctive composite mark.

Recommended positioning: treat **Wrestling Manager** primarily as a descriptive/sub-brand beneath THE TEAM MANAGER rather than relying on it as the company's strongest standalone trademark.

## Clearance still required before filing

A comprehensive search should include:
- USPTO Trademark Search for exact, phonetic, plural/singular, spacing, and formative variants;
- live federal applications/registrations in related software/SaaS/sports-management goods and services;
- Wyoming and other relevant state trademark databases;
- corporate/business-name databases;
- Apple App Store, Google Play, web domains, social handles, SaaS products, and common-law users;
- logo/design similarity once final artwork is selected.

## Filing strategy to discuss with trademark counsel

Potential marks:
1. THE TEAM MANAGER — standard characters, if counsel believes registrable.
2. THE TEAM MANAGER + distinctive logo — design/composite application.
3. Distinctive future house mark, if adopted, with THE TEAM MANAGER used descriptively underneath.
4. Sport-specific composite logos rather than trying to monopolize "Wrestling Manager" alone.

Potential goods/services should be drafted around the actual product, likely including downloadable mobile software, downloadable software for sports-team administration/scoring/communications, and/or SaaS services. Exact classes and identifications should be selected using the current USPTO ID Manual.

## Usage rules

- Use ™ now for a mark you claim as a trademark.
- Do not use ® unless and until the specific mark is federally registered.
- Use the mark consistently as an adjective/source indicator, not merely as the generic name of the service.
- Preserve dated screenshots, launch records, invoices, App Store listings, website captures, and marketing materials as evidence of first use and continuous use.

## Market evidence noted

Current products using wrestling-manager wording include mobile wrestling simulation titles and older tournament-management software. This increases the need to distinguish the scholastic/competitive team-management product by source branding, visual identity, and goods/services description.
