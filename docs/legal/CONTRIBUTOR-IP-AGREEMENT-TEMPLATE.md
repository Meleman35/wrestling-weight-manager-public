# Contributor / Contractor IP Agreement — Draft

**Use before any outside developer, designer, photographer, videographer, consultant, or contractor contributes material. Attorney review recommended.**

Company: **Mele Sports Technologies LLC**  
Contributor: ______________________________  
Project: The Team Manager and related products

## 1. Work product

"Work Product" means code, designs, graphics, documents, specifications, photos, video, audio, inventions, improvements, tests, schemas, and other materials created specifically for the Project under this engagement.

## 2. Ownership and assignment

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Work Product specially commissioned as a work made for hire is a work made for hire for Company. To the extent any Work Product does not qualify as work made for hire, Contributor irrevocably assigns to Company all transferable right, title, and interest in that Work Product, including copyrights and patent rights.

## 3. Pre-existing materials

Contributor must identify in writing any pre-existing or third-party material before incorporating it. Contributor grants Company only the license specifically listed for approved pre-existing material. No copyleft or source-disclosure-triggering component may be introduced without prior written approval.

## 4. Third-party rights

Contributor will not knowingly incorporate material that infringes another party's copyright, trademark, patent, privacy, publicity, contract, or other rights.

## 5. Open-source software

Contributor must provide the package name, version, source URL, license, and intended use of every new third-party software dependency. GPL/AGPL/SSPL or other reciprocal/source-availability licenses require explicit written Company approval before use.

## 6. Confidentiality

Nonpublic source code, product plans, credentials, athlete/family data, business information, security details, and unreleased features are confidential and may be used only for the engagement.

## 7. Credentials and data

Contributor may not copy production personal data into personal devices, public repositories, AI services, test fixtures, or external services except as expressly authorized. Secrets must use approved secret-management systems and may not be committed to source control.

## 8. Further assurances

Contributor will reasonably assist with confirming Company ownership and executing documents needed for registration or enforcement.
