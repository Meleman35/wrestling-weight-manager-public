# Founder Intellectual Property Assignment — Draft for Signature

**This document is prepared for review and signature. It is not executed merely by being stored in the repository.**

Effective date: __________

Assignor: Damon Mele ("Assignor")  
Assignee: **Mele Sports Technologies LLC**, a Wyoming limited liability company ("Company")

## 1. Assignment

For good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Assignor hereby assigns to Company all right, title, and interest that Assignor owns in and to the business and software project commonly known as **The Team Manager**, including its sport-specific products such as **Wrestling Manager**, together with all transferable intellectual-property rights associated with them.

## 2. Included assets

The assignment includes, to the extent owned by Assignor:

- source code, object code, scripts, native applications, web applications, database schemas, migrations, APIs, tests, documentation, specifications, designs, user-interface layouts, workflows, scoring systems as expressed in software, and original written materials;
- original logos, icons, graphics, audiovisual material, marketing material, and brand assets;
- domain names and websites, including theteammanager.app, subject to registrar transfer requirements;
- trademark rights and associated goodwill in THE TEAM MANAGER and other marks owned by Assignor;
- copyrights and copyright applications or registrations;
- trade secrets, know-how, product plans, confidential technical information, and inventions;
- contractual rights to commissioned or licensed materials to the extent transferable;
- claims for past, present, and future infringement to the extent assignable by law.

## 3. Exclusions

This assignment does not transfer third-party software, open-source components, licensed fonts, APIs, platform technology, sports-organization trademarks, or other property not owned by Assignor. Such materials remain governed by their applicable licenses or permissions.

## 4. AI-assisted materials

To the extent Assignor owns transferable rights in AI-assisted output, those rights are included. Nothing in this agreement asserts copyright in purely machine-generated material where copyright law does not recognize human authorship. Human-authored selection, arrangement, modifications, code integration, specifications, testing decisions, documentation, and other protectable contributions are included.

## 5. Further assurances

Assignor agrees to execute reasonable documents needed to confirm Company ownership, record assignments, transfer domains or accounts where appropriate, or prosecute intellectual-property registrations.

## 6. Representations

Assignor represents, to the best of Assignor's knowledge, that Assignor has authority to assign the rights described here and has not knowingly assigned the same rights to another party.

## 7. Governing law

Wyoming law governs this agreement except where federal intellectual-property law controls.
