# Parent / Guardian Consent — Product Draft

**Draft for counsel review and implementation. Verifiable-parental-consent requirements depend on the child's age, service design, information collected, and applicable law.**

## Parent/guardian notice

The Team Manager / Wrestling Manager allows teams and families to manage athlete participation, profiles, communications, wrestling records, photos/video, schedules, forms, and related team activities.

For younger athletes, a parent/guardian may be asked to authorize creation or activation of the athlete profile and control selected sharing and communication settings.

## Information that may be associated with a child

Depending on enabled features: name, photo, age/birth information, school/grade, team memberships, guardian relationships, competition records, attendance, weight/weigh-ins, messages, photos/video, forms, clothing/gear information, and clearance records.

## Parent controls

Where applicable, a verified parent/guardian should be able to:
- review the child's profile and important privacy settings;
- approve or reject profile photos and designated public/recruiting fields;
- control profile discoverability/following and eligible messaging;
- control athlete participation in optional sharing features;
- review or receive safety-related communications as designed;
- request access, correction, export, or deletion of the child's personal information;
- revoke optional consent and stop future collection for the affected feature.

## Consent statement

By completing the approved parent/guardian verification and consent process, I confirm that I am authorized to act for the identified child and consent to the collection, use, and disclosure described in the then-current child/privacy notice for the features I enable.

I understand that:
- required team/competition records may differ from optional social/profile features;
- I may change optional settings later;
- withdrawal of optional consent does not necessarily erase legitimate records already required by a team, organization, law, safety obligation, or completed transaction;
- I can contact support or use in-product privacy controls for questions and requests.

## Implementation requirements before relying on this consent

- record the consenting adult, child/profile, notice version, purpose/features, method, timestamp, and verification method;
- provide the parent a copy/receipt;
- allow consent withdrawal;
- prevent child self-bypass;
- separate required operational processing from optional profile/social/recruiting/video permissions;
- re-consent when materially expanding collection/use beyond the original notice;
- do not use a checkbox alone where law requires a stronger verifiable parental consent method.
